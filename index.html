Quick Engineering Reference

Core relations used during the practical sessions.

V = I × R
P = V × I
Series Resistance: RT = R1 + R2 + ...
Parallel Resistance: 1/RT = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + ...
RC Time Constant: τ = R × C
RL Time Constant: τ = L / R
Transformer Ratio: Vs/Vp = Ns/Np
Three-Phase Power: P = √3 × VL × IL × pf
